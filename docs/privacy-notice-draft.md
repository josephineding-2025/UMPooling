
# UMPooling — User Privacy Notice (Draft for Team Review)

**Owner:** Member 6
**Status:** NOT FOR PUBLICATION — requires verification of actual data handling, legal requirements and contact details

## Proposed in-app notice

**Privacy and safety information**

UMPooling is a university coursework prototype intended to help verified members of the Universiti Malaya community coordinate shared commuting. We aim to collect and use only the information needed to operate the relevant features and evaluate the prototype.

**Information processed.** Depending on the features you use, the prototype may process your UM email address, profile role, faculty or department, residential area or college, vehicle details if you offer rides, saved routes, ride requests, bookings, trip records, messages, ratings and estimated commuting impact. A trip record may associate the driver and booked passengers with trip times, vehicle registration and origin/destination zones. The SOS feature may use current location only when requested with the necessary permission and technical availability.

**Why information is used.** These details support account verification, ride matching, booking and trip coordination, trip safety, user ratings and aggregated climate-awareness statistics. The prototype should not collect student identification card photographs.

**Who may see it.** Access to identifiable trip and booking data is intended to be limited to authorised participants and specifically approved administrative roles. Ordinary commuters should not be able to view unrelated users' private trips. Authorised sustainability/analyst views should use anonymised aggregated figures rather than individual journey histories. Exact access controls must be validated before this notice is published.

**Emergency messages.** If you activate SOS, UMPooling proposes to prepare a message with available trip details and, when permission is granted and current location can be obtained, a location link. You may choose SMS or WhatsApp and review the message before sending. Sending through an external application is under your control; its own terms and privacy practices apply. Merely opening a message composer does not mean the message has been sent or that assistance has been dispatched. The emergency recipient(s) have **not yet been approved**.

**Location and permissions.** Where location access is needed, the app should request the appropriate device permission and explain the reason. If permission is denied, the proposed SOS flow will continue without current location. This notice does not authorise continuous background tracking.

**Retention.** Identifiable trip, booking, message, rating and SOS retention periods have **not yet been finalised**. Before release, UMPooling must state approved retention durations and how records are deleted or anonymised, including any justified restrictions for safety incidents.

**Your choices and requests.** The final prototype should explain how a user can correct profile information and ask about access, correction or deletion of personal data, subject to the approved operating arrangements. The team must provide a verified contact or support channel before publishing this notice.

**Further information.** This draft requires project-team/lecturer review and verification against the actual deployed Android and Supabase implementation and any applicable Malaysian or UM privacy requirements. It must not be described as proof of compliance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010.

## Outstanding decisions before publication

- Identify the actual data controller/operator, contact channel and user-support process.
- Verify whether any analytics, push notification, authentication and external messaging service receives data, and what data is transferred.
- Approve retention periods, disposal methods and any incident-related holds.
- Confirm whether SOS records are created automatically, what location is stored, and who can read or resolve them.
- Confirm permitted users of identifiable trip records and anonymous aggregate dashboards.
- Determine applicable legal/institutional notice, consent and rights requirements through appropriate review.
- Ensure final English and Bahasa Melayu UI copy matches the approved implementation.

**Release gate:** No final user-facing notice until the above items are resolved and the actual data flows have been checked.
